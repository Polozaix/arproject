## Writing approach

Write short factual notes during development, then organise them into an argument once the formal scope is agreed. The report should explain why the design was chosen and what the evaluation demonstrates, rather than describing every script or recounting each day of development.

Treat the first clock-sway experiment as formative developer feedback. It can motivate a design change, but cannot establish a general user preference. Keep claimed results proportional to the number of testers, tasks, and conditions actually evaluated.
